# Section 10: Borrowing a deep network

GOVT 6039, Fall 2026 · Lectures 18 and 19 · 18 November 2026

Can a network trained by someone else measure the tone of parliamentary speech, and when should we trust its numbers? We use XLM-R, a 24-layer pretrained network, on sentences from the UK, Bosnian, Croatian, Serbian and Czech parliaments (ParlaSent 1.0).
